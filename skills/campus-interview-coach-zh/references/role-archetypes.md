# Role Archetypes

Use this file when the JD is short, vague, or mixes several functions.

## Product / AI product / technical PM

Likely signals:
- problem definition
- prioritization
- user understanding
- cross-functional communication
- tradeoffs between speed, quality, and scope

Second-round pressure:
- why this problem matters
- how you decide priority
- how you align engineering, design, and business

## Strategy / business analysis / CEO office

Likely signals:
- structured thinking
- issue framing
- information synthesis
- hypothesis building
- concise communication

Second-round pressure:
- whether the candidate can think in causes, not just observations
- whether they can move from data to recommendation

## Operations / user growth / merchant / platform ops

Likely signals:
- execution discipline
- data sensitivity
- process building
- cross-team coordination
- ownership on messy problems

Second-round pressure:
- whether the candidate understands business levers
- whether they can connect actions to operational outcomes
- whether they can prioritize under resource constraints

## Marketing / brand / content / community

Likely signals:
- user insight
- content or campaign logic
- channel understanding
- coordination and landing ability
- ability to read feedback loops from data or comments

Second-round pressure:
- whether the candidate understands why content or campaigns work
- whether they can explain audience, message, channel, and measurement

## User research

Likely signals:
- research design
- interview or survey rigor
- synthesis quality
- actionable insight generation
- communication with stakeholders

Second-round pressure:
- whether the candidate knows when not to overclaim
- whether insights were translated into decisions, not just reports

## Data analyst / business analyst

Likely signals:
- metric understanding
- SQL or data-tool fluency if relevant
- problem framing
- insight extraction
- communication with non-technical stakeholders

Second-round pressure:
- whether analysis changed a decision
- whether the candidate understands data limitations and tradeoffs

## Generic rule

If the role family is unclear:
- infer the top 3 verbs in the JD
- infer the top 3 business nouns in the JD
- infer who the role serves internally or externally
- decide whether the team is hiring for `doing`, `deciding`, or `aligning`

Then tailor the mock or analysis accordingly.
