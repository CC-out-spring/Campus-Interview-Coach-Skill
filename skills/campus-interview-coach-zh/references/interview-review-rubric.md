# Interview Review Rubric

Use this file when the user asks:
- `帮我打分`
- `这轮我有机会过吗`
- `评估一下通过率`
- `这场面试表现怎么样`

This rubric is for structured, defensible postmortems. It is not a fake-scientific promise of certainty.

## Core rule

The score must be evidence-based.

Every judgment should be tied to:
- a specific answer the user gave
- a specific signal the interviewer was likely testing
- a specific standard for that round

If evidence is incomplete, say the score is provisional.

## Recommended scoring dimensions

Use 5 to 7 dimensions depending on the round. Do not force all dimensions if they were not observed.

Default dimensions:
- `表达与结构`
- `项目硬度与真实性`
- `ownership / 主动性`
- `业务判断`
- `岗位理解 / 动机匹配`
- `产品或行业理解`
- `成熟度与抗压`
- `follow-up resilience / 抗追问`

For technical roles, replace or de-emphasize `产品或行业理解` when not relevant.

For live training, prefer 3-5 observable dimensions. Do not use every dimension if it distracts from re-answering.

## Round-specific weighting

### First round

Prioritize:
- expression clarity
- resume credibility
- concrete ownership
- whether the candidate can execute

Do not over-weight:
- macro business judgment
- executive-level strategic framing

### Second round

Prioritize:
- judgment
- prioritization
- role fit
- business understanding
- whether the hiring manager can defend the HC decision

Common second-round drag factors:
- only execution, no thinking
- generic motivation
- weak understanding of the platform / business / team
- answers that are valid but not at the right level

### HR / final behavioral round

Prioritize:
- consistency
- motivation-fit and choice standards
- values fit
- values under tradeoff
- communication maturity
- risk profile
- commitment cost: timeline, location, offer intention, compensation expectations, and role reality
- reverse-question quality when the user had a chance to ask questions

Do not score HR/final rounds only by whether the user sounded polite. A safe but generic answer may still be weak if it fails to reveal why the candidate is choosing this role and what realistic constraints they understand.

## Score anchors

Use these anchors to avoid score inflation.

For training, also classify the answer's quality level:

### Training quality levels

- `Level 1: 答到问题`: the answer is relevant but may be plain or thin.
- `Level 2: 有证据`: the answer includes concrete facts, actions, or examples.
- `Level 3: 有判断`: the answer explains why, tradeoffs, priority, or method.
- `Level 4: 有匹配`: the answer connects the story to the target role, team, or round.
- `Level 5: 抗追问`: the answer can survive challenges about ownership, motivation, metrics, tradeoffs, and consistency.

Use the level to decide the next drill. Do not push pressure follow-ups if the answer is still below Level 2.

### 90-100
- clear hire signal
- strong answers across most critical dimensions
- few obvious weak spots
- likely top-tier relative to normal campus pool

### 80-89
- strong or upper-middle performance
- several convincing answers
- still has some weak spots, but overall competitive
- often `有机会过，而且不低`

### 70-79
- mixed but viable
- enough strengths to stay in contention
- clear weaknesses remain
- typical wording: `有机会，但不是稳过`

### 60-69
- below target for that round
- some credible material, but not enough consistently
- usually needs a weaker comparison pool or strong interviewer goodwill

### Below 60
- multiple clear misses
- weak alignment with the round's bar
- low pass likelihood unless recap is missing key positive evidence

## Pass-likelihood guidance

Use ranges, not single-point estimates.

Example mappings:
- `85+`: often `70%-85%`, depending on team bar and competition
- `78`: often `55%-65%`
- `72`: often `35%-50%`
- `65`: often `15%-30%`

Adjust the range up or down based on:
- how competitive the target team likely is
- whether the role is urgent and hard to fill
- whether the interviewer asked deep follow-ups
- whether the user only gave a partial recap
- whether HR/final-round risk points were clarified or left unresolved
- whether reverse questions showed role awareness or only transactional concern

## Suggested response structure

Use this order when the user asks for a score:

1. `综合评分`
2. `通过概率`
3. `为什么是这个分`
4. `最强的地方`
5. `最危险的地方`
6. `关于反问 / 面试氛围 / 收尾信号的解释`
7. `如果我是面试官，我会怎么想`
8. `下一步建议`

For live training, use a shorter order:
1. `这题考点`
2. `最强点`
3. `最大风险`
4. `下一版只改一件事`
5. `请重答`

## Language rules

Good phrasing:
- `基于你提供的内容，我会给一个偏审慎的判断`
- `这更像可过可挂区间`
- `你不是明显挂相，但也不是面完就稳`
- `这题方向没错，但层级还差半级`
- `这轮不是考你会不会说好听话，而是在校准你会不会做成熟选择`
- `你的回答很安全，但没有暴露选择标准，所以 HR 很难判断稳定性是真的还是临场表态`
- `这个反问不算错，但它只问个人收益，没有体现你对岗位现实的理解`

Avoid:
- fake certainty
- empty reassurance
- overclaiming hidden hiring knowledge

## Example diagnostic phrases

Use concise labels:
- `项目是成立的`
- `表达顺`
- `有 owner 雏形`
- `平台理解不够深`
- `更像靠谱执行型，还没完全打到值得花 HC`
- `这题会让面试官心里打问号`
